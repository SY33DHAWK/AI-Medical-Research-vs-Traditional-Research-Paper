# 🧠 AI Medical Research vs Traditional Research Paper Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **comparative text analysis** between **AI-assisted medical research papers** and **traditional human-written medical research papers**. The objective is to identify linguistic, structural, and stylistic differences between the two writing approaches.

The analysis is conducted using **R and RStudio**, where research papers are divided into two distinct corpora:
- **Foreground Corpus** → AI-generated / AI-assisted medical research texts  
- **Background Corpus** → Traditional, human-written medical research texts  

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Build two separate text corpora for comparison  
- Analyze vocabulary usage, sentence structure, and complexity  
- Identify stylistic differences between AI-generated and traditional research writing  
- Explore how AI impacts academic medical research writing  

---

## 🧪 Methodology
1. Collection of medical research papers  
2. Corpus construction:
   - Foreground corpus (AI-assisted papers)
   - Background corpus (Traditional papers)
3. Text preprocessing (tokenization, cleaning, normalization)
4. Comparative text analysis
5. Interpretation of linguistic patterns and trends  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **R**
- **RStudio**
- Text analysis libraries such as:
  - `tm`
  - `quanteda`
  - `tidytext`
  - `ggplot2`
